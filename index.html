A few things to know.

Is this designed for 3PLs?

Yes. We focus on picking and transport in small and mid-size warehouses, including 3PL operations with multiple clients and changing order volumes.

Do we need to buy the robots?

packsel’s model is leasing. Fleet size, term, implementation, and support are defined after reviewing your operation.

Can this work with our WMS?

We review your system’s API, order data, item locations, and scanning requirements. Compatibility is confirmed during assessment.
